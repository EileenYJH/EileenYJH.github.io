NEXA is a proof-of-concept AI middleware layer sitting between SAP S/4HANA
and e2open GTM. It classifies HS tariff codes with a confidence score,
matches the best of 17 Malaysian free-trade agreements, computes full landed
cost, and routes every result through mandatory human sign-off before it's
written back to SAP — with an immutable audit trail behind every figure.

Built by team NEXA (Eileen Yeoh, Eavan Tan, Jessy Pang, Xin Yuan, Chua Xin
Ying) for Jabil's IT ECP Bootcamp 3.0 — **Champion, Use Case 2**.
